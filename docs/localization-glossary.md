## Voice and formatting

- Friendly, concise and reassuring; avoid bureaucratic language in actions and errors.
- Use Arabic digits for digital data in all three locales.
- Do not translate API codes, IDs, URLs, scientific names, standards or legal identifiers.
- Currency follows the transaction currency; language changes presentation, never value.
- Newly generated Thai or Chinese copy remains **Needs native review** until reviewed by a qualified speaker in screen context.
