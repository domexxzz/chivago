# 57 — Koh Sichang

Thai version: [`57-koh-sichang-th.md`](57-koh-sichang-th.md).

What the public record says about Koh Sichang's problems, read against the
ones the Samui pilot was built for. Desk research only, gathered 19 October
2026 from news and official releases; nobody from the team has been on the
island for it. Every claim below carries its source. Where the record is
silent, this says so rather than filling the gap.

## Why Sichang

It is the nearest island to the campus work (`43-the-campus.md`): a short ferry
from Sriracha, a day trip from Bangkok, and a place the province is already
spending on. If ChivaGo is going to show it is not a Samui-only product, the
second island should be one where the same problems exist at a smaller scale
and the people who own them are reachable.

## The problems, set against Samui

| Problem | Samui | Sichang | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh water | Severe shortage | Supply reaches households by truck | Strong |
| Waste and wastewater | Yes | Litter on beaches, a wastewater system still to be built | Strong |
| Crowding at peak times | Concentrated at a few beaches | Ferry queues on long weekends | One report |
| Pollution from boats | Some | Heavy ferry and freight traffic from Sriracha | Medium, sources dated |
| Plans that do not reach the ground | — | A provincial crackdown that stalled | Medium, sources dated |

### 1. Fresh water

- The Permanent Secretary of the Interior visited to follow up on household
  water shortage under the royal policy; the Department of Public Works and
  Town & Country Planning starts with a pilot upgrade of the water supply
  network. ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/news/595464))
- The municipality trucks groundwater to households without a meter: more
  than 8,400 deliveries and over 16,800 tonnes between January and mid-December
  2025. The same release says the island's main sources are still adequate.
  ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/royal-policy-project-advances-sustainable-development-on-koh-sichang-529827))

So the official position is a distribution problem rather than a supply one.
That is a claim to check on the ground, not to repeat.

### 2. Waste and wastewater

- Officials have described rubbish-strewn beaches and oil slicks from sunken
  vessels. ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/marine-dept-asked-solve-pollution-around-koh-sichang-172308))
- The island's development master plan has five workstreams and 54 projects,
  including a central wastewater collection and treatment system with reuse of
  treated water. ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/royal-policy-project-advances-sustainable-development-on-koh-sichang-529827))
- In May 2026 the Minister of Natural Resources visited to push work on water,
  waste, energy and environmental restoration for sustainable tourism.
  ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/local/east/2812767),
  [Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/local/966214))
- At least one guesthouse has replaced single-use plastic with glass bottles,
  cloth bags and free refills.
  ([Greenpeace Thailand](https://www.greenpeace.org/thailand/story/24456/plastic-trash-hero-srichang/))
  This is the kind of operator who could be a first host.

### 3. Crowding

- More than 379,000 Thai and foreign visitors between January and mid-December
  2025. ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/royal-policy-project-advances-sustainable-development-on-koh-sichang-529827))
- On the May 2026 long weekend the ticket queue at Ko Loi pier in Sriracha ran
  close to a kilometre. ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/local/962039))

### 4. Pollution from boats

- Passenger and supply boats from Sriracha add air pollution; oil from fishing
  boats has drifted to Bang Saen and Pattaya and killed marine animals.
  ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/marine-dept-asked-solve-pollution-around-koh-sichang-172308))
- A coal carrier, SE1, sank about 500 m off Ko Ran Dok Mai; the Department of
  Marine and Coastal Resources monitored the coral and water quality.
  ([Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/289223))
- The master plan includes controls on vessels carrying goods from the mainland.

### 5. Plans that do not reach the ground

- Chonburi announced an environmental crackdown on boat operators and
  businesses ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/news/environmental-crackdown-set-for-koh-sichang-136944));
  eight months later the province was still talking rather than acting
  ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/news/chonburi-talking-not-walking-koh-sichang-environmental-problems-163357)).

## Where ChivaGo fits

- **Problem 5 is the product.** The island does not lack plans; it lacks a
  record that a specific thing happened on a specific day with somebody
  standing behind it. That is what a host-verified Green Point and an issued
  statement are (`31-the-evidence-layer.md`). A beach clean or a refill station
  becomes something the municipality, a sponsor or a hotel can point to.
- **Problem 3** is the dispersal the quests were designed for: a quest away
  from the pier and the palace on a busy weekend, with the crowding shown on
  the same screen.
- **Problem 2** gives first hosts who already exist: the plastic-free guesthouse,
  and whoever runs beach cleans.
- **Problems 1 and 4** are not things the app can fix. At most it can show the
  island's condition honestly, the way the Samui conditions card does, if a
  measured source exists.

## What the record does not show

- Coal dust from transshipment off the island. The coal-dust reporting found
  is about Nakhon Luang (Ayutthaya) and Mahachai (Samut Sakhon), not Sichang.
- Road safety (the island's samlor and motorbike traffic), drowning, or
  any coral restoration programme specific to Sichang.
- Residents' and operators' own views. Most sources are government releases or
  Pattaya Mail, and some Pattaya Mail pieces are several years old.

## Before this goes in a deck

1. Ask Koh Sichang municipality and the DMCR Chonburi office for current
   figures on water, waste and visitors, and cite those instead of the press.
2. Find two or three would-be hosts on the island and ask whether they would
   verify activity in their own name. Without a named host the product has
   nothing to issue.
3. Re-date or drop the Pattaya Mail items on boat pollution and the stalled
   crackdown once their publication dates are confirmed.
