About ChivarGo

ChivarGo is a wellness-travel app for islands and campuses in Thailand. It puts places, local shops, live sea and sky conditions and safety tools on one screen, in Thai, English and Chinese.

Places and local shops

Where to go, reviews from people who actually went, and shops run by locals.

Today's weather and sea

Temperature, rain, wind and waves from Open-Meteo, cross-checked against the Thai Meteorological Department's forecast, plus air quality.

Green Points

Points earned when a named host verifies an activity you did on the spot.

Travel parties

Plan a trip together, chat as a group, and share your location when you choose to.

SOS

Sends your location to the emergency contacts you picked, with 1669 and the Tourist Police (1155) a tap away.

Chivar Bot

A travel assistant that answers only from the app's own data and never invents a figure.

Our rule: a point is only worth something if somebody had to check it, and every number in the app can say where it came from.
